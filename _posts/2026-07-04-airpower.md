After destroying my flight computer in my previous rocket in 2025 I've decided to take another shot in subminimum builds.


# <span style="color:#fff;">Goals</span>

- Break the sound barrier
- Recover flight data and video 

<br><br>

# <span style="color:#fff;">Technical Details</span>
I don't exactly remember why but I've settled on 44mm diameter for some reason. From the start of the project I knew that it will be a 100% composite build.
After I calmed down after Meteor Festival 2025 I've started working. I choose conical nosecone made from fibreglass, in which there was 3D printed electronics 
sled. For the avionics I picked flight proven PTR Tracker, and new small flight computer made by Baltic Space. Under these boards, I put Runcam Split 4 v2 camera, 
which is a new thing in my rocket projects. For the airframe I originally went with fibreglass tubes, but after many failed attempts and money poured down the drain (skill issue)
I bought carbon fiber one. Tube works as a rocket airframe and rocket motor casing, which basically makes it single use, but I do not plan to fly this more than once.
Motor simulations showed that it would deliver about 560Ns of impulse and 370 newtons of thrust for 1.5s, thus accelerating the rocket to Mach 1.5 (1850km/h or 514m/s at sea level).
